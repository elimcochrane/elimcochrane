## Hi, I'm Eli!

I'm a computational social psychology researcher at the University of Kansas. I'm interested in exploring political psychology, group identity, and meaning-making via natural language processing. I'm especially curious about how group identification informs individuals' social and political realities.

Read more about my research interests on my [academic portfolio website](https://elimcochrane.github.io/) and connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/elicochrane/)!
